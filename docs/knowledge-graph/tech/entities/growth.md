# Growth

**Type:** concept

Growth is a multifaceted subscription product, service plan tier, and implementation model that appears across different business contexts, serving both as a digital billing plan and a comprehensive sales machine package. Within the Stripe platform, Growth is configured as a final catalog plan offering multiple instances, campaigns, funnels, and expanded contacts, and is utilized for Hubflow billing alongside a 30-day implementation timeline. 

In its capacity as a complete sales machine package and service tier, Growth is highlighted as the primary and most chosen hero implementation plan for stores with a monthly revenue ranging from R$ 60,000 to R$ 150,000. It is prominently featured with a standalone purchase button and is priced at R$ 5,497, with an alternative payment option of 12 installments of R$ 549, contributing to an ecosystem client lifetime value (LTV) of approximately R$ 8,800. 

As a comprehensive service model, the Growth plan encompasses a 30-day implementation schedule and includes a wide array of business assets and features such as audits, ad campaigns, video recording sessions, group platforms, Instagram funnels, sales scripts, team training, and post-delivery monitoring.
