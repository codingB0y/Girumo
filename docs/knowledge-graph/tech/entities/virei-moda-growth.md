# Virei Moda Growth

**Type:** organization

Virei Moda Growth is an organization, brand, and operating entity associated with the landing page header (vireimoda-lp) and serves as the data controller for the privacy policy page, based in Goiânia, Goiás. Functioning as a growth program, service, and sales machine installer, Virei Moda Growth specializes in scaling wholesale fashion operations significantly within the Região da 44 (polo da 44) in Goiânia. 

The entity offers a specialized 30-day implementation process designed to scale wholesale stores from R$ 5 thousand to R$ 350 thousand per month, which includes installing vending or sales machines in wholesale fashion stores. Additionally, Virei Moda Growth incorporates a design token system featuring a price-markdown aesthetic inspired by the 44 and utilizes an official logo with the descriptor "Growth." The organization is also associated with digital offerings, including the facilitation of a free X-ray request feature within its application.
