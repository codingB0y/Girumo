# Meta

**Type:** organization

Meta is a multifaceted technology and advertising platform, as well as a parent company that governs services such as WhatsApp and Instagram. As a corporate parent, Meta manages WhatsApp's privacy policy, connection fingerprints, and anti-automation detections, while also serving as the official creator and provider of the WhatsApp Cloud API and Groups API. Additionally, the entity provides essential developer credentials, including the INSTAGRAM_APP_ID and INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET.

In the digital marketing domain, Meta operates as a comprehensive advertising platform that features automated rules for campaigns, such as managing CBO budgets and shutting down underperforming ad sets. The platform provides the manager for ad kits, guides, and is also associated with advertising operations and account blocks. Furthermore, Meta offers external services like the Meta Pixel, which can be integrated within landing pages for tracking and analytics purposes.
