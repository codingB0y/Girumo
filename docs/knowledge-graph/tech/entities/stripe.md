# Stripe

**Type:** organization

Stripe is a comprehensive financial services and payment processing platform utilized by Hubflow to manage critical billing infrastructure. It serves as the primary engine for handling complex payment operations, including the administration of products, pricing models, and multi-tenant billing requirements. By integrating with the platform, Stripe enables the seamless management of customer subscriptions and recurring revenue cycles.

Beyond core payment processing, Stripe functions as a vital integration point for event-driven architectures. It is responsible for managing webhooks, which ensure that Hubflow remains synchronized with real-time financial events and status updates.
