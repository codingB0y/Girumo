# HubFlow

**Type:** organization

HubFlow is a multi-tenant SaaS platform designed to enhance business operations through advanced automation and communication services. At its core, the platform specializes in WhatsApp automation, providing merchants with comprehensive tools for lead management and efficient WhatsApp group operations intended to drive commercial results.

To facilitate sophisticated business workflows, HubFlow integrates AI-specialized agents capable of assisting with various operational tasks. The platform architecture includes robust administrative tools that allow for the management of tenants, individual users, and these AI agents. Financial operations and subscription management within the platform are handled through an integration with Stripe.

The development of HubFlow has been an iterative process, progressing through distinct phases ranging from V1 to V5. Throughout its lifecycle, the platform has remained under the strategic oversight and management of the Product Council, ensuring its continued evolution as a centralized solution for automated communication and enterprise administrative efficiency.<SEP>A multi-tenant SaaS platform for WhatsApp-based lead acquisition and marketing automation.<SEP>A SaaS platform designed for wholesale clothing businesses to manage WhatsApp groups and sales.
